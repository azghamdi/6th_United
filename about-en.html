Forum overview

A global platform
for better data

The Forum is a space for practical dialogue, knowledge exchange and partnerships that help data create measurable social, economic and environmental impact.

The sixth edition in Riyadh reflects the Kingdom’s growing role in the global statistical community and connects international priorities with national transformation and innovation.

Forum themes

Four pathways
towards impact

The thematic areas connect innovation, trust, value and partnership to build inclusive and resilient data ecosystems.

Innovation for better and more inclusive data

Using new sources, technologies and methods to improve data quality and respond to changing user needs without leaving anyone behind.

  • Innovation
  • Inclusion
  • Use and reuse

Maximising data use and value

Making data findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable while strengthening communication and statistical literacy.

  • FAIR principles
  • Communication
  • Data literacy

Building trust, protection and ethics

Embedding ethics throughout the data lifecycle, protecting privacy and confidentiality, and strengthening trust in official statistics.

  • Data ethics
  • Privacy
  • Trust

Effective partnerships and a fairer ecosystem

Strengthening coordination, sustainable investment and resilient national data systems that maintain essential services during crises.

  • Coordination
  • Investment
  • Resilience
Frequently asked questions

What to know
before attending

For the latest organisational information, please refer to the official registration page.

When and where will the Forum take place?＋

The Forum will take place from 9 to 12 November 2026 at the Sofitel Riyadh Hotel & Convention Centre in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

How can I register?＋

Registration is available through the United Nations Indico platform and requires an account and an attendance application.

Does submitting an application guarantee attendance?＋

No. Applications are subject to review, and attendance is confirmed only after official approval is received.

Who is the Forum for?＋

It is designed for data producers and users, national statistical offices, policymakers, technology and geospatial experts, academia, civil society and the private sector.

What is Road to Riyadh?＋

A series of preparatory events highlighting Saudi statistical transformation and strengthening international dialogue on data for sustainable development.

Is the final programme confirmed?＋

The current programme is preliminary. Rooms, times and session details may change, so attendees should check the official programme before travelling.
