Frequently asked questions

What to know
before attending

For the latest organisational information, please refer to the official registration page.

When and where will the Forum take place?＋

The Forum will take place from 9 to 12 November 2026 at the Sofitel Riyadh Hotel & Convention Centre in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

How can I register?＋

Registration is available through the United Nations Indico platform and requires an account and an attendance application.

Does submitting an application guarantee attendance?＋

No. Applications are subject to review, and attendance is confirmed only after official approval is received.

Who is the Forum for?＋

It is designed for data producers and users, national statistical offices, policymakers, technology and geospatial experts, academia, civil society and the private sector.

What is Road to Riyadh?＋

A series of preparatory events highlighting Saudi statistical transformation and strengthening international dialogue on data for sustainable development.

Is the final programme confirmed?＋

The current programme is preliminary. Rooms, times and session details may change, so attendees should check the official programme before travelling.
